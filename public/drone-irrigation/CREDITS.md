# Field Pilot Assets

Soil: [Brown Mud Leaves 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_leaves_01), Poly Haven.
Grass: [Aerial Grass Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock), Poly Haven.
Tree: [Island Tree 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_02), Poly Haven. Geometry simplified and textures resized to 512 pixels/WebP for the browser.
Sky and environment: [Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy Pure Sky](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky), Poly Haven.

The bundled terrain maps, tree model and textures, and HDR environment are [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).
Drone geometry, farm structures, crop leaves, grass blades, terrain shape, water effects, and flight audio are generated in the application.
