# 2. The Thesis

Purpose: Explain the belief behind the platform before explaining the mechanics.

**SECTION LABEL**

The idea

## The best venture may already be hiding inside a problem.

Companies encounter problems every day. Students spend years developing knowledge, skills and the ability to think. What is often missing is the bridge between the two.

Udyaan creates that bridge.

A problem becomes a challenge. A challenge finds the right builders. Builders work with mentors and problem owners. The strongest solutions are tested in the real world. What proves its value can move beyond the project and toward adoption, research, intellectual property or a new venture.

## From academic work to work that matters.

The goal is not to simulate the real world. The goal is to work inside it.

# 3. Why Udyaan Exists

Purpose: Describe the gap in the existing student and company ecosystem.

## Students should not have to wait until graduation to discover what they can build.

A degree can teach a student how to understand a subject. Real work teaches them how to make decisions when the answer is not already in the textbook.

Companies, meanwhile, are surrounded by operational problems, unmet customer needs and opportunities that do not always fit inside a conventional hiring or consulting process.

Udyaan creates a working environment where these two worlds can meet early.

Students get real problems to work on. Companies get a structured way to access fresh thinking and build solutions. The university becomes the platform that connects capability, mentorship, knowledge and execution.

# 4. What Is Udyaan?

Purpose: Define the product simply and concretely.

## Udyaan is where problems become projects, projects become proof, and proof can become ventures.

Udyaan is a university-led platform for problem solving, applied learning and venture creation.

It is designed around a simple loop: bring in a meaningful problem, find people capable of solving it, give them the support to build, test the solution in context, and create a path for the work to continue.

## Not a classroom. Not a competition. Not a one-time project.

Udyaan is a continuous build-and-validation environment. The work starts with a real problem and is judged by what the team can prove.

# 5. The Udyaan Model

Purpose: Show the complete system as the central visual and explain each stage.

## The Udyaan engine

Problem → People → Team → Build → Validate → Outcome → Venture

### 01. Problem

A company, industry partner, researcher or student brings forward a problem worth solving.

### 02. People

Students discover the problem and apply based on the skills, curiosity and capability they can bring.

### 03. Team

The right capabilities come together around the problem. Cross-disciplinary teams can combine technical, creative, business and domain strengths.

### 04. Build

The team researches the problem, frames the opportunity, creates a solution and works with mentors along the way.

### 05. Validate

The solution is tested with users, evidence, technical requirements and commercial reality in mind.

### 06. Outcome

The work can be adopted by the problem owner, continued as research or IP, or taken forward by the student team.

### 07. Venture

When there is a validated opportunity and a builder willing to continue, the project can become the foundation for a new venture.

# 10. The Build Journey

Purpose: Explain the execution process as the core product experience.

## From problem statement to working solution.

### 01. Understand

Learn the context before trying to solve it. Understand the user, the system, the constraints and what has already been attempted.

### 02. Investigate

Talk to the people closest to the problem. Gather evidence. Test assumptions. Find the real opportunity inside the original challenge.

### 03. Frame

Turn a broad problem into a clear, testable problem statement and define what a useful solution would need to achieve.

### 04. Build

Create the first version. It may be a prototype, process, product, model, service or system - whatever best tests the idea.

### 05. Test

Put the solution into a real context and observe what happens.

### 06. Iterate

Use evidence to improve the solution. Keep what works. Change what does not.

### 07. Validate

Build enough evidence to answer the most important question: is this worth taking forward?

### 08. Present

Show the problem, the approach, the evidence, the outcome and the next step.

# 11. Mentor + Company Loop

Purpose: Make external involvement visible as part of the build, not an optional add-on.

## You build. Experts challenge. The problem owner responds.

Udyaan brings the team into regular contact with people who understand the problem from the inside.

### Mentor

### Problem Owner / Company

### Student Team

The loop is simple: Build → Get Feedback → Improve → Test Again.

# 12. Validation

Purpose: Create a strong transition from project work to venture-grade evidence.

## Proof before promises.

A good presentation is not the finish line. The solution needs evidence.

### Technical validation

Can it work? Can it be built reliably within the stated constraints?

### User validation

Does it solve a real need for the people who would use it?

### Market validation

Is there a credible need, customer or adoption pathway?

### Commercial validation

Can the solution create enough value for someone to pay for it, adopt it or support its continued development?

## The decision

Continue. Iterate. Adopt. Research. Spin out. Not every project becomes a company - but every serious project should leave with a clear next step.

# 24. FAQ

Purpose: Answer practical questions that reduce uncertainty before application or partnership.

## About Udyaan

### What is Udyaan?

Udyaan is a university-led venture-building platform where real problems are matched with student builders, supported by mentors, and taken through a structured build-and-validation process.

### Is Udyaan an internship, an incubator or a venture programme?

It can function across those stages. Students can enter through a real problem, build and validate a solution, and eligible work can continue toward company adoption, research/IP or venture creation.

## For Students

### Do I need my own startup idea to join?

No. You can enter by choosing a real problem from the Udyaan platform.

### Can students from different disciplines work together?

Yes. Team composition is driven by what the problem requires.

### How are students selected?

Selection focuses on capability, reasoning, curiosity, ownership, collaboration and the ability to work through uncertainty.

### Can a project become my own venture?

A validated opportunity may be taken forward as a student-led venture, subject to the relevant programme, university and partner arrangements.

## For Companies

### Can a company submit a problem?

Yes. Companies can bring real problems for the platform to frame, match and work on.

### Does the company decide the solution?

The company provides the problem context and constraints. The student team is expected to investigate and build the solution.

### What happens if the company likes the solution?

The next step can be adoption, pilot, partnership, continued development or another agreed pathway.

## IP and Ownership

### Who owns the solution or intellectual property?

Ownership depends on the specific project, contributor roles, agreements and applicable university or partner terms. Each project should establish the relevant terms before substantial build activity begins.
